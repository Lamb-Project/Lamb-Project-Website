These guides cover what **LAMB 0.9** adds. 0.9 is a release candidate: it is on the development branch and has not been tagged.

For everything else, such as creating, testing and publishing assistants, knowledge, LAMB LEGATUS, Moodle and LLM access, the [0.8 guides](/en/guides/0.8/) remain the reference. Where 0.9 behaves differently from what they describe, the [0.9 release notes](/en/releases/0.9/) say so.
