## Earlier documentation

There are no archived editions in this collection yet. The 0.8 guides are the current edition and stay at their version-specific addresses when a newer edition takes their place. An archived guide does not, by itself, mean that the feature it describes has been deprecated.

The version label identifies the guide's scope. Availability still depends on your institution's installed revision and configuration. [Read the availability notes](/en/availability/).
