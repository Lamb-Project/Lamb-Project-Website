The Creator screenshots in these tutorials were captured from **LAMB 0.8 on 17 October 2026**. They show the real application, including its own v0.8 label. Interface text, version badges and responses have not been redrawn or replaced.

| Image group | Capture date | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Assistant creation, properties and chat | 17 October 2026 | A synthetic teacher creates an assistant and receives a completed response |
| Knowledge bases and RAG configuration | 17 October 2026 | A demonstration document is uploaded, processing completes, its passage is retrieved, and a new Simple Rag assistant selects the source |
| Test cases and results | 17 October 2026 | A case is saved and run against the RAG assistant; the captured response is available for review |
| Learning-tool template | 17 October 2026 | The public template connects to LAMB 0.8; instructor and learner launches use a local demonstration LMS, and the learner receives a new assistant response |

The examples use demonstration accounts and synthetic course material. No institutional Moodle course or real learner submission was accessed for these captures. Model names, local addresses and identifiers are examples, not configuration values to copy into your institution.

The tool template has its own interface and version history. Its screenshots show that application connected to LAMB 0.8. The [template guide](/en/guides/0.8/learning-tool/) identifies its source revision and the known grade-display limitation. No new grade was sent to the demonstration LMS during this capture run.

You can open each tutorial image at full size. These captures document the demonstrated steps and responses; they do not establish general educational effectiveness or acceptance on another institution's LMS.
