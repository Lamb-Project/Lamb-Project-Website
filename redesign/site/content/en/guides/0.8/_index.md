Start with a teaching task. These guides cover LAMB 0.8, including the existing assistant-publication and Creator-access paths. No existing entry path is designated deprecated in this edition.

The website is a **local review edition for the 0.8 launch**. Creator screenshots were captured from LAMB 0.8 on 17 October 2026; the tool-template images show its connection to that instance. Check your installation's version and the [screenshot notes](/en/guides/0.8/screenshots/) when controls differ.

## Before you start

Your institution supplies a LAMB address or a course link, an appropriate account and access to approved models. There is no shared public sign-up service on this website. [Help for students](/en/students/) explains the learner route.
