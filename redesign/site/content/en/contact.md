## Request a demonstration

Tell us which teaching use case or workflow you would like to explore, who would attend and whether your institution already uses LAMB. The team will discuss the next step with you.

## Prefer to explore independently?

Start with the [0.8 installation guide](/en/guides/installation/) or the [illustrated teaching guides](/en/guides/0.8/).

## Already using LAMB?

For account access, course permissions or a problem with your institution's service, contact your local support team. For reproducible software issues, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/Lamb-Project/lamb/issues). Keep credentials and private course content out of public reports.
