LAMB is free software: any institution can install and run it without asking us. We, **Marc Alier and Juanan Pereira**, created LAMB. For institutions that want help, we offer three services: training, consultancy, and talks (keynotes and seminars).

To ask about any of them, write to **contact-lamb-project@proton.me**. Tell us your institution, your role, which service you are interested in, your LMS, any dates you have in mind and, if you already run LAMB, which version. We answer together.

## Training

Three strands, which can be combined:

- **For teachers:** creating and testing course assistants, preparing knowledge bases from course materials, working with LAMB LEGATUS (the assistant built into LAMB that helps teachers set up and test their assistants), and adding LAMB activities to Moodle courses.
- **For administrators, operators and support teams:** installation, organizations and users, AI model providers, the Moodle connector and day-to-day operation.
- **AI governance, for leadership, data protection and support staff:** what the Safe AI in Education Manifesto asks of an institution, the duties the EU AI Act and the GDPR place on your institution, AI literacy for staff, and which of those duties LAMB settings can support and which stay with the institution.

## Consultancy

For institutions adopting LAMB, or AI in teaching more broadly, that want to decide who is responsible for what before students use it.

- **A first conversation and a demonstration** of the use cases you have in mind.
- **Pilot design:** choosing the course, the team, the learning purpose and how the pilot will be evaluated.
- **Deployment planning:** where to run LAMB (your own servers or a contractor's), LMS integration, choice of AI model provider, and cost planning.
- **AI governance:** who may create and use AI activities, which providers and data are approved, how conversations may be accessed, and how students are informed. We help you make these decisions and record them; compliance stays with the institution.

## What we do not offer

We do not host or run LAMB for institutions, and we do not provide operational support or service-level agreements. Your institution runs its LAMB service, directly or through a company it contracts.

## Companies offering LAMB services

Companies that want to host, support or integrate LAMB for institutions can write to the same address.

## Prefer to explore independently?

Start with the [0.8 installation guide](/en/guides/installation/) or the [illustrated teaching guides](/en/guides/0.8/).

## Already using LAMB?

For account access, course permissions or a problem with your institution's service, contact your local support team. For reproducible software issues, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/Lamb-Project/lamb/issues). Keep credentials and private course content out of public reports.
