LAMB is free software: anyone can install it, run it and build on it without asking us. LAMB was created by **Marc Alier and Juanan Pereira**.

The LAMB team offers:

- **Training** for educators, developers and the people who run LAMB, including AI governance.
- **Consultancy** for institutions, teaching teams and development teams adopting LAMB or AI in teaching, including AI governance.
- **Keynotes and seminars** on safe AI in education, AI governance and LAMB.

Write to **contact-lamb-project@proton.me**. Tell us who you are, what you would like to do with LAMB and any dates you have in mind.

## Training

Strands that can be combined:

- **For educators:** creating and testing course assistants, preparing knowledge bases from course materials, working with LAMB LEGATUS (the assistant built into LAMB that helps educators set up and test their assistants), and adding LAMB activities to Moodle courses.
- **For developers:** building learning tools on LAMB with the creator API, LTI and the [tool template](/en/developers/), and working with the LAMB and Moodle command-line tools.
- **For administrators and operators:** installation, organizations and users, AI model providers, the Moodle connector and day-to-day operation.
- **AI governance, for leadership, data protection and support staff:** what the Safe AI in Education Manifesto asks of an institution, the duties the EU AI Act and the GDPR place on it, AI literacy for staff, and which of those duties LAMB settings can support and which stay with the institution.

## Consultancy

For institutions, teaching teams and development teams adopting LAMB, or AI in teaching more broadly.

- **A first conversation and a demonstration** of the use cases you have in mind.
- **Pilot design:** choosing the course, the team, the learning purpose and how the pilot will be evaluated.
- **Deployment planning:** where to run LAMB, LMS integration, choice of AI model provider, and cost planning.
- **Building on LAMB:** designing a learning tool or integration around LAMB assistants.
- **AI governance:** who may create and use AI activities, which providers and data are approved, how conversations may be accessed, and how students are informed. We help you make these decisions and record them; compliance stays with the institution.

## Hosting and support

The LAMB team does not host LAMB or run it as a service. We are in contact with companies that do, and if you need hosting, integration or operational support, we can refer you to LAMB integrators.

## Prefer to explore independently?

Educators can start with the [illustrated teaching guides](/en/guides/0.8/), developers with the [developer route](/en/developers/), and operators with the [0.8 installation guide](/en/guides/installation/).

## Already using LAMB?

For account access, course permissions or a problem with your institution's service, contact your local support team. For reproducible software issues, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/Lamb-Project/lamb/issues). Keep credentials and private course content out of public reports.
